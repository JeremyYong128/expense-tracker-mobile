# Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker Mobile

**Effective Date:** August 2026

## Introduction
This Privacy Policy applies to the "Expense Tracker Mobile" application (hereby referred to as "the App"). The App was built with a strict local-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your financial data is exclusively yours.

## Data Collection and Usage
**We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your data.** 

The App operates entirely locally on your device:
* **Local Storage:** All your transactions, categories, and financial data are saved directly to your device's internal storage using a local SQLite database. 
* **No Cloud Servers:** The App does not connect to any external cloud databases or servers to back up or process your data.
* **No Analytics or Tracking:** We do not use third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporters, or advertising trackers that harvest your personal information or usage habits.

## Third-Party Access
Because your data never leaves your device, no third-party individuals, companies, or services have access to your financial information through the App. 

## Data Retention and Deletion
Your data is retained on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You have full control over your data:
* You can delete individual transactions or categories from within the App at any time.
* If you uninstall the App, your device's operating system will automatically and permanently delete the local database containing all of your financial records.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. If future updates to the App introduce opt-in cloud backups or receipt-parsing features, this policy will be updated to reflect exactly how that data is handled. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. 

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
